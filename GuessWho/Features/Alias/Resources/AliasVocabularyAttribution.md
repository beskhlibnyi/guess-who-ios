# Alias Ukrainian vocabulary attribution

This app bundles the scored SQLite deck generated by `bin/build_alias_words`
from `mph_ua.db` and UberText 2.0's lemma frequency dictionary. The importer
normalizes Ukrainian headwords, removes stress markers, deduplicates entries,
and excludes proper names, phrases, abbreviations, and non-noun/verb/adjective
entries from the default pool. Rare or unmatched words remain available with a
higher difficulty score.

Source: `mphdict` by uSofTrod (2017)
https://github.com/LinguisticAndInformationSystems/mphdict
